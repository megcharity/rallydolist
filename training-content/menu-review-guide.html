Menu
Review Guide

Our menu is big and it changes with the seasons — you won't learn all of it in one day, and you don't need to. Today your MOD will guide you through three things: the whole menu at a glance, a deep dive on three dishes, and our allergen sheet. Write everything in your own words. The goal is that a guest coming off the court trusts your recommendation.

How to use this guide
1
Walk the full menuGo through every section with your MOD and fill in the Menu Map (page 2) — one line per dish. Know what each one is, not everything about it.
2
Deep dive on 3 dishesYour MOD assigns three dishes using the roles below. Fill out a Dish Profile (page 3) for each, and taste whenever you can.
3
Learn the allergen sheetYour MOD walks you through our allergen sheet and allergy process (page 4). Practice finding answers fast.
