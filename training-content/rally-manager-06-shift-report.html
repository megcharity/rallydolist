The Shift
Report

The shift report is Rally's record of what happens every day. It gives the whole leadership team visibility, whether or not they were on shift. A well-written report takes 10 minutes and tells the full story of the shift.

The Rally Standard

Write it like someone who wasn’t there needs to understand the shift. Because they do.

How to complete it right

Own your block

  • The AM manager completes the AM section before their shift ends
  • The PM manager completes the PM section at close
  • Neither waits for the other, and neither leaves without it done

No blank fields

  • If a field doesn't apply, write N/A
  • A blank field looks like something was missed. N/A means it was considered and intentionally skipped

Coaching & feedback

Never blank.

  • If you're on the floor, you're coaching
  • Smooth shift? Recognize great work. Something slipped? Address it
  • No coaching moment by the end of the shift? Create one: find something to celebrate and something to sharpen

Specific notes

“Good shift” tells no one anything.

  • Programming and activations: what worked, what didn't, how guests responded
  • Player and captain feedback worth passing up
  • Operational issues: equipment, service gaps, anything that affected guests
  • Wins: standout team members and great guest moments
If it's not documented, it didn't happen. A coaching conversation that isn't written down can't be referenced if a pattern develops later.

Event feedback

For every event that ran on your shift, connect with the POC in person before they leave. Not by text or email afterward.

Ask every POC
  • “What did you enjoy most tonight?”
  • “What's one thing we could improve for next time?”
  • “Would you come back, and is there anything that would make it an easy yes?”
Fill in the Events section immediately, while it's fresh. The Director of Sales reviews this section, so write it accordingly. Couldn't find the POC? Note it in the report and follow up by phone or email the next morning.

Comps & discounts

Document every comp and discount on the shift with a note: who, why, and who approved it.

Every comp
  • Who received it
  • Why
  • Who approved it
Every open % use
  • The guest or check it was applied to
  • The reason
  • The percentage
  • The authorizing manager's name
No undocumented open % discounts. If it's in the Toast report and not in your shift report notes, you will be asked about it.

Sending it

Hitting Submit Full Report is the last thing the closing manager does before locking up. The report emails itself the moment you submit it.

  1. Finalize the full shift reportEvery field complete, with N/A where it doesn't apply, including your Positive and Negative Comps totals pulled from Toast.
  2. Hit Submit Full ReportThe system sends the recap to the Ops Managers, F&B Director, Sales Director and Exec Chef automatically. No screenshot, no PDF, no email to compose.
  3. Need to fix something after you submit?Edit the report and use Resend Email to send the corrected version.
The standard doesn't change based on how busy the shift was. A hard night is more reason to document, not less.
