Side Work
at the Host Desk

The host desk is the first and last stop for every guest. Side work keeps it ready: calls returned, members expected, paddles prepped, and the desk spotless. This chapter walks you through each part, including the calls you make whenever the desk is quiet.

The Rally Standard

Down time is never idle time. If you're not actively helping a guest, you're working through your side work.

Retention is a connection

Retention outreach isn't busywork. It brings guests back to Rally, and it tells us what we're doing well and what we could do better.

Your Side Work Checklist

Four areas to keep on top of every shift. If anything is unclear, ask your manager.

1

Communications & front desk

Goal: nothing waiting on a reply.

  • Check for missed calls, and call back every one
  • Check your "Things to Know" sheet; ask your manager if you have questions
  • Open the hello@rally inbox and respond to every email — use AI to help with grammar — and copy the GM on every email and response
  • Teams-message the right person when a package arrives for them
2

Guest & member prep

Goal: know who's coming before they walk in.

  • Look up the day's members
  • Write their time of visit and prepare their welcome sign
  • Update the members list
  • Make sure all Courts & Coffee and Rally Social players are paid for
  • Make sure all players are checked in
  • Check the floor chart: know which Captain is on which court
3

Cleanliness & organization

Goal: a host desk that looks ready for guests.

  • Tidy the shelves: organized and clean
  • Wipe down the host desk (no rings)
  • No boxes at the host desk; get them to the right owner
  • Keep the host closet organized
  • Keep lost and found up to date and organized
4

Paddles & equipment

Goal: every court gets clean, ready equipment.

  • Wipe down all paddles in the box, then put them back
  • Prepare paddle bags for courts: four paddles and two balls per bag
4 + 2

Four paddles and two balls in every paddle bag.

CC: GM

Copy the GM on every hello@rally email and response.

Down-Time Tasks

These are mandatory. Whenever you're not actively helping a guest, work through these calls.

Task 1

Retention efforts

Rally always has a retention effort running — reconnecting with guests who haven't been back, or following up with first-timers. The tactic and the incentive change over time, so ask your manager what the current effort is before you start.

  • Confirm what you're doing: calls, emails, or both
  • Confirm the current incentive, so you quote it correctly
  • Leave a note whenever you make contact, so the team knows who's been reached
Task 2

Respond to emails

Log in to the hello@rallypickleball.com inbox and respond to every email waiting. Use AI to help with grammar, and copy the GM on every email and response.
