Side Work
Around the Venue

Swarmers run and buss across the whole venue, connecting front of house to back of house. Clean tables, full waters and stocked captains' quarters keep service moving. This chapter walks you through how we open, run and close the venue.

The Rally Standard

Know every task and the standard it has to meet, then exceed it. If you're ever unsure about a task, ask the MOD. We're here to help.

Always on the move

A swarmer is never standing still. Every lap is a chance to buss a table, refill a water, run dishes or spot a guest who needs something. Your work keeps the captains free to take care of guests.

How a Shift Flows

Swarmer side work happens in three parts. Here's what each part is for and every task it covers.

1

Opening

Goal: the floor is stocked and set before the first guest.

  • Open the buss cart (flip the bin carts)
  • Help stock captains' quarters for service: plates, cups, carafes and rolls
  • Set up the outside lounges: menus, QR codes, water, cups and cold towels
  • Check tables for stickiness and water marks
  • Make sure the lounges are crumb-free
  • Fill up the water in the lounges
  • Wipe down the drink trays
2

During the shift

Goal: keep moving. Every lap leaves the venue cleaner than you found it.

  • Do continuous laps around the venue: sweep, wipe down tables, and buss tables and courts
  • Reset setups and rolls with plates as tables turn over
  • Clean all surfaces, including side tables
  • Keep waters full and cold towels stocked: lounges, the cafe water station and outside
  • Wipe down menus and reset them in the lounges
  • Run dirty dishes to the kitchen throughout the shift
  • Put the correct F&B menus on all courts
  • Spot sweep as needed
  • Polish silverware and glassware (from the kitchen or the bar)
  • Discard broken glass
  • Restock plates
  • Refill rolls in the pizza kitchen and C4 captains' quarters, and tidy as needed
  • Remove outdoor couch cushions once the last reservations leave; straighten indoor couch pillows
3

Closing

Goal: the opener walks into a clean, stocked venue.

  • Help captains clean the dining area tables
  • Sweep the venue so floors are free of food and debris
  • Help clean the captains' quarters work surfaces
  • Stock captains' quarters to par: paper supplies, glassware, plateware, sauces and more
  • Roll silverware and fold napkins (the lead captain or MOD gives the count)
  • Take out all trash, indoors and outdoors
  • Break down and discard any boxes
  • Clean the trash cans
  • Make sure all handhelds are accounted for and charging
  • Tell management about any facility issues: wobbly tables, lightbulbs out, broken handhelds and more
  • Dump any mop water and empty the buckets
  • Check out with the MOD (the lead captain signs your checkout first)
  • All other duties as assigned by the MOD
Laps

Continuous laps all shift: sweep, wipe, buss.

Par

Stock captains' quarters to par at close.

2 sign-offs

Lead captain signs your checkout, then check out with the MOD.

Words to Know

Terms you'll see on the checklist and hear on the floor.

Captains' quartersThe captain service stations, stocked with plates, cups, carafes and rolls. There's one in the pizza kitchen and one at C4.
ParThe set amount of each item a station should have. Stock captains' quarters to par at close.
Rolls / rollupsSilverware rolled in a napkin. At close, roll to the count the lead captain or MOD gives you.
SetupsA table's full reset: rolls and plates in place, ready for the next guests.
Buss cartThe cart for bussed dishes. Open it at the start of the shift by flipping the bin carts.
HandheldsThe handheld devices the team uses on the floor. All of them get accounted for and put on charge at close.
