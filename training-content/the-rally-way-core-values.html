Part 3

Magic Moments

There is an important distinction: great service is expected. A Magic Moment goes beyond the expected.

Doing your job well

Refilling water. Greeting a guest. Clearing dirty dishes. Cleaning a table. Giving accurate directions.

These matter. They are part of excellent hospitality.

Creating a Magic Moment

You notice something about a guest and act on it in a thoughtful, personal, unexpected way.

It doesn't have to cost money. It requires paying attention.

Examples: connect a nervous new player with someone in their group, remember a regular's favorite drink, recognize a celebration, or write an encouraging note on a coffee sleeve.
