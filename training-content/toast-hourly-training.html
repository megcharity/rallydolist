Hourly Team Training

Toast should make service faster, not harder. The goal is simple: open the right check, ring the right items, communicate important details, send accurately, and close the right check.
Part 1 · System Basics

Start clean. Start correctly.

Before speed comes accuracy. You should be able to log in, clock in, navigate the home screen, switch between the areas you use, and find menu categories without guessing.

1
Log in.

Turn on the terminal or handheld and enter your 4-digit passcode.

2
Select your role.

Choose the correct job role when prompted.

3
Clock in.

If Toast prompts you at the start of your shift, complete the clock-in.

4
Know your screen.

Practice finding the floor plan, New Tab/New Check, menu categories and the functions you use during service.

NEW CHECK = GUEST COUNT

Every time you create a new check, enter the correct guest count. Every check. Every court. Every table. Even during events.

COURTS & TABLES

Learn the map

Know which floor-plan tab contains the court or table you need. Do not guess during service.

NEW CHECK

Enter guest count

The guest count is part of creating the check. It is not optional and should never be skipped because the building is busy or because you're working an event.

BAR TAB

Name it clearly

For a bar guest, use New Tab and enter the guest's name clearly so teammates can identify the correct check.

Part 3 · Ringing Orders

Accuracy before Send.

Once a check is open, choose the category, select the item, complete the required prompts and modifiers, review the check, then Send.

1
Choose the category.

Food, drinks or the appropriate menu section.

2
Select the item.

Add the correct item to the check.

3
Add modifiers.

Complete item-specific prompts and customizations such as “No Tomato” or “Add Cheese.”

4
Review before Send.

Catch mistakes before they become kitchen or bar problems.

5
Send.

Fire the order to the appropriate production area. Send drinks promptly so the bar can get started.

PAUSE BEFORE YOU HIT SEND: Is the item right? Are the modifiers right? Is it on the right check?

Part 4 · Allergies

Allergies are not just a note.

ALLERGY STANDARD

If a guest mentions an allergy, use the allergy-specific modifier, enter the allergy in RED ALL CAPS, and notify the kitchen in person. Do not assume the Toast note alone is enough.

Part 5 · To-Go Orders

Set the dining option first.

1
New Check.
2
Set Dining Option to To-Go.

Do this before selecting the items.

3
Add the guest's name.

Use a clear label such as “Mike S - To-Go.”

4
Ring and modify normally.

Add relevant notes such as sauce on side, no utensils or extra napkins.

5
Send.

Fire the ticket to the kitchen.

Part 6 · Payments & Splits

Close the right check.

Payment accuracy matters. Before completing a payment, confirm you are working on the correct guest's check.

Card

Tap Pay, choose Card, and follow the prompts. On a handheld, hand the device to the guest when appropriate.

Cash

Choose Cash and enter the amount the guest gives you. Toast will calculate the change due.

Split Check

Tap the three dots / More, then Split Check. Move selected items to new checks or use Split Evenly. Each resulting check can be paid separately.

Part 7 · Voids, Comps & Corrections

Know when to get a manager.

BEFORE SEND

If you made an order-entry mistake before sending, select the item and use the appropriate Remove/Void function and reason.

AFTER SEND

Once an item has been sent, a manager is required to void or comp it. Don't improvise a workaround. Get a manager and explain what happened.

Part 8 · End of Shift

No mystery checks left behind.

1
Review your checks.

Use Shift Review to identify anything still open.

2
Close or transfer.

Every open tab must be appropriately closed or transferred to another team member.

3
Clock out.

Once your shift responsibilities are complete, use your name/menu to Clock Out.

Part 9 · Hands-On Practice

Knowing it isn't the same as doing it.

During training, practice these workflows on the terminal or handheld. Trainers should introduce complications and make trainees complete the full workflow.

SCENARIO 1

Simple Service

SCENARIO 2

Bar Tab

SCENARIO 3

Allergy

Ring a modified food order, correctly flag a severe allergy, and demonstrate the verbal kitchen notification.

SCENARIO 4

To-Go

Set the dining option, name the order, add items and modifiers, and Send.

SCENARIO 5

End of Shift

Find open checks, determine which need to close or transfer, and complete the shift review.

Training is complete when the team member can perform the workflow accurately, not just describe it.
