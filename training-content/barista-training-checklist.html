Barista Training
Checklist

Everything a Rally barista needs to know, in one checklist. Sit down with your manager and work through it together. When every item is reviewed and you can do it on your own, you're ready for the cafe.

The goal

Review every item with a manager until you know how to do all of it, on your own, with confidence.

1 · Review it

Your manager walks you through the item: tells you, shows you, then has you try it. Check Reviewed.
