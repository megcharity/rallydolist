HotSchedules Training for New Associates

Reviewed Oct 2026

Welcome to HotSchedules

HotSchedules is the app we use to post the schedule, collect your availability, and handle time-off and shift-swap requests. By the end of this training you should be able to do four things on your own:

  • Log in on your phone and on the website
  • Find your schedule
  • Set your weekly availability
  • Request a day off

Your manager makes the final call on every request. Nothing you submit is official until it shows as approved.

Getting started

You can use HotSchedules two ways: the mobile app or the website, hotschedules.com. Both use the same login, which your manager will give you on your first day.

  1. Get your login. Ask your manager for your username and temporary password.
  2. Choose app or web. The app is on the Apple App Store and Google Play. HotSchedules lists it at $2.99. If you'd rather not buy it, log in at hotschedules.com from your phone's browser instead; everything in this guide works there too.
  3. Log in and change your password.
  4. Check your contact info. Make sure your phone number and email are correct so you get schedule updates.
  5. Turn on notifications in the app so you hear about schedule posts and request approvals.

Viewing your schedule

Your schedule is the first screen you see when you open the app. Check it every time a new schedule is posted, and again before each shift in case something changed.

  • App: open HotSchedules; your upcoming shifts show on the home screen. Use the menu to see the full store schedule.
  • Website: log in at hotschedules.com from any browser.
  • Email or text: you can have your schedule sent to you.
  • Phone: HotSchedules runs a call-in line, 866-753-3853, if you can't get online.
You are responsible for knowing your shifts. "I didn't check the app" is not an excuse for a missed shift.

Setting your availability

Availability is your standing weekly pattern: the days and hours you can work every week. Set it in your first week, because new accounts start out as available 24/7 until you change them.

You only submit it once. It stays in effect until you create a new one, so there is no need to resubmit weekly. To change it later, you create a new availability; you can't edit the old one.

On the mobile app (iPhone or Android)

  1. Open the menu and tap My Availability.
  2. Tap the add (+) icon to start a new request.
  3. Pick the date the new availability should start, then tap Done (iPhone) or OK (Android).
  4. Tap each day of the week and choose one option: Available (any time that day), Unavailable (can't work at all that day), or Partially Available (enter exact hours).
  5. When every day is set, tap Save (iPhone) or the check icon (Android).
  6. Type a Reason for Change, pick your approving manager, and tap Submit.

On the website

  1. Log in at hotschedules.com.
  2. Go to Settings, then Personal.
  3. Click the Availability + icon.
  4. Choose the date the new availability starts and type a reason for the change.
  5. Click and drag on the weekly grid to mark the times you're available or unavailable.
  6. Pick your approving manager if asked, then click Submit Availability.

After you submit

Your request shows as "pending approval" and isn't official until a manager approves it. You'll get a message in HotSchedules when it's approved. To pull back a pending request, tap the start date's icon and choose Cancel Request.

Tip: Have a class or second job that changes by season? You can set an availability with a future start date (for example, the first day of spring semester) so it switches over automatically.

Requesting a day off

Use a time-off request when you normally work a day but can't work one specific date, like a doctor's appointment, a wedding, or a trip. Don't change your availability for a one-time conflict.

Availability vs. time off

You need to...UseExample
Block the same days or hours every weekAvailability"I have class every Tuesday until 3 pm"
Skip one date or a few datesTime-off request"I'm out Saturday, Nov 14 for a wedding"
Get out of a shift already on the posted scheduleRelease or swap the shift, and tell your manager"I'm scheduled Friday but now can't make it"

Request types you may see

  • Request Off — ask not to be scheduled that day. The manager decides when building the schedule.
  • Request to Work — ask to be scheduled on a date.
  • Unpaid Time Off / Paid Time Off — formal requests that a manager approves or denies.

Not every type is turned on at every location. Ask your manager which one we use.

How to submit it on the app

  1. Open the menu and tap Time Off & Requests.
  2. Tap the add (+) icon.
  3. Choose the request type.
  4. Pick the date or date range. Leave All Day Off on for the full day, or turn it off to enter specific hours.
  5. Add a short comment explaining why.
  6. Tap Done (iPhone) or SAVE (Android).

To change or delete a request, open it from Time Off & Requests and tap Edit or the trash can (iPhone), or the pencil icon (Android).

On the website

Log in at hotschedules.com, go to the Home tab, and create a new request there. The fields are the same as in the app.

What happens next

A request is not approved until a manager approves it. Until then, plan to work if you're on the schedule. Submit requests as early as you can, ideally before the schedule is written.

Dropping, swapping, and picking up shifts

If the schedule is already posted and you can't work a shift, drop it or swap it. A dropped shift is posted to the team in HotSchedules so anyone can pick it up.

If no one picks it up, it is still your shift and you are expected to work it. Every drop, swap, and pickup also needs a manager's approval before it's final.
  • Drop a shift (give it away): Tap the shift on your schedule, choose Release, and add a reason. You can release it to the whole team or send it straight to one coworker, who can accept or decline. Keep checking the app; until someone picks it up and a manager approves, plan to come in.
  • Swap a shift (trade): Tap your shift, choose Swap, pick the coworker's shift you want, add a reason, and tap Request Swap. Your coworker accepts first, then a manager approves.
  • Pick up a dropped shift: Tap Available Pickup on your schedule to see shifts coworkers have dropped. Choose one and tap Request Pickup. It's yours once a manager approves.
  • Pick up a house shift: A house shift is an open shift the manager has posted with no one assigned to it. When it's open for pickup, it shows in Available Pickup next to dropped shifts. Tap it, then Request Pickup, and wait for manager approval.

The Available Pickup button shows a badge with the number of new open shifts, so check it when you want extra hours. Shifts waiting for approval show as a grayed-out dotted box on your schedule, and you'll get a HotSchedules message when a request is approved or denied.
