Employee relations

  • Address performance, conduct, attendance and employee relations concerns promptly
  • Partner with HR before issuing written warnings, final warnings, suspensions or termination recommendations
  • Document specific facts, conversations, expectations and outcomes. Submit all disciplinary documentation to HR for the personnel file
  • Report harassment, discrimination, retaliation, workplace violence, threats, safety concerns and other policy violations to HR immediately
  • Follow the coaching process in Chapter 8

Attendance documentation

  • Verify attendance facts before taking corrective action
  • Avoid assumptions. Confirm the right attendance category before deciding next steps
  • Unexcused absences are documented and addressed through corrective action, in partnership with HR
What isn't a no call, no show: an employee who notifies management of an absence has not committed a no call, no show, even if the absence is unapproved.

Timekeeping

The rule
  • Everyone clocks in when they start work and out when they finish, including salaried exempt managers
  • Leaving the property during your shift, for a business errand (picking up liquor or supplies) or a personal reason? Clock out when you leave and back in when you return
  • Record only your own time
Why it matters
  • Rally knows who's onsite in an emergency
  • It verifies staffing coverage, including opening and closing duties
  • It keeps attendance records accurate
  • It's not used to calculate pay for salaried exempt employees
Clocking in or out for someone else, or falsifying time records, may result in corrective action.

Form I-9

This is a legal requirement. How you ask for documents matters.

Don't
  • Tell employees which ID documents to bring
  • Give examples like a driver's license, passport, Social Security card or birth certificate
Do
  • Give the employee the USCIS Lists of Acceptable Documents
  • Let them choose which acceptable document or combination to present
Use language like this

“Please sign and return your offer letter via email. Please also bring documentation for your Form I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification. Review the Lists of Acceptable Documents and bring either one document from List A or one document from List B together with one document from List C.”
uscis.gov/i-9-central/form-i-9-acceptable-documents

Associates may not begin training until their I-9 has been submitted and verified in iSolve. See Chapter 10.

Manager communications

  • Use GroupMe for business-related communication only
  • Keep group membership accurate, and remove employees promptly when they leave
Never discuss these in group chats: investigations, corrective action, medical information, leave requests, compensation, or any other confidential employee matter. Move sensitive conversations offline or involve HR.

Payroll deductions & uniforms

  • Payroll deduction and uniform agreements are part of onboarding, and may also be given to current employees when needed
  • They explain employees' responsibilities for company property, including uniforms, and when deductions may be considered under applicable law
  • Employees are not charged for normal wear and tear
Questions about deductions, damaged property or unreturned property? Consult HR before you communicate expectations to the employee.
