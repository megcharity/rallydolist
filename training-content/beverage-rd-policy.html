R&D Policy

Developing new drinks is part of the job — and it comes with strict rules. North Carolina law and our permit depend on every one of us following them exactly. Read this chapter carefully and review it with your MOD.

The short version

R&D happens only when the MOD approves it, while you're clocked in. Nobody swallows the product.

The Four Rules

Every R&D session, every time.

1

Get it approved

  • The MOD approves every session in advance: date, time, who, and which drinks.
  • You're clocked in and paid. Never off the clock.
  • 21+ only. Never during service.
  • Had a drink today? No R&D today.
2

Taste. Spit. Dump.

  1. Dip a clean straw in and cap the top with your finger.
  2. Lift it out and release a few drops on your tongue.
  3. Spit into the spit cup. Never swallow.
  4. New straw every taste. Dump the drink right away.

No full drinks. No sips from the glass. No leftovers.

3

Don't make it for others

  • Make R&D drinks only for an approved session.
  • A coworker asks outside a session? Say no and tell the MOD.
  • Making the drink is a violation, not just drinking it.
4

Log every drop

  • Every alcoholic ingredient and exact amount goes on the R&D log.
  • Hand the log to the MOD before you leave.
  • The MOD rings it into Toast at $0, same day.
  • Not logged = not authorized.
NC law

If you swallow any alcohol here, even a small taste, you must be off duty for the rest of the day, out of uniform, and do no more work. Swallow = clock out. (14B NCAC 15B .0209)

Who Owns What

Everyone involved in R&D has a part — including managers.

Bartender (hourly)

  • Only approved, scheduled R&D
  • Clocked in the whole time
  • Taste, spit, dump
  • Log every ingredient and amount
  • Refuse and report unapproved requests

Manager on Duty

  • Approve and schedule every session
  • Confirm everyone is clocked in and 21+
  • Watch every taste and dump
  • Review the log; ring it into Toast at $0 same day

Managers doing R&D

  • Same rules: taste, spit, dump, log
  • Approved by the GM or bar manager, never yourself
  • Another manager reviews your log
  • Never the MOD during your own R&D
  • Swallow anything? You can't run the floor today

Why We're Strict: NC Law

Our permit depends on it.

  • Drink on the premises = done for the day. Staff who drink here must be off duty for the rest of the day, out of uniform, and do no work. (14B NCAC 15B .0209)
  • No cocktail tasting exception. NC lets staff sample only new beer and wine products (2 oz each, 8 oz a day). Spirits and cocktails aren't covered.
  • No one may be intoxicated on the premises, staff included. (.0209)
  • Only employees 21+ may mix liquor drinks. (.0203)
  • Liquor records must be accurate. Product that leaves inventory unsold has to be recorded with a reason. (.0509, .0515)
  • Rally is responsible for every employee's actions. One violation hits our permit. (.0201)

Violations

These can lead to discipline, up to termination, for everyone involved — including whoever made the drink:

  • Unapproved or off-the-clock R&D
  • Swallowing, finishing, or keeping drinks
  • Making a drink for unapproved R&D
  • Not logging product

No exceptions without written approval from ownership.

Not sure? Ask your MOD before you pour.
