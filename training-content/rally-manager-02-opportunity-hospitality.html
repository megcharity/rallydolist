Hospitality

Every regular started with a first visit. Not every guest walks through the door the same way, so your job is to read who's in front of you and set your team up to meet each guest exactly where they are.

The Rally Standard

Every guest in front of you is an opportunity to build the Rally community. Treat every single one like they could become your best regular, because they could.

Five guest types

Five types of guest, five opportunities. Each one needs something different.

01

First-time players

Win them over for life, starting right now.

  • Identify them early (“Is this your first time at Rally?”) and make a warm, big deal of it
  • Walk them through check-in, how courts are set up, how RallyTrack works, and where the café and bar are
  • Share programming, events and memberships without overwhelming them
  • Check in mid-visit more than you would with a regular
  • Warm send-off, plus something worth coming back for
02

Out-of-town guests

Make Rally part of their Charlotte story.

  • Acknowledge it: “Welcome to Rally and to Charlotte!”
  • Take a genuine interest in where they're from and what brought them here
  • Be their concierge: restaurants, things to do, places to see
  • Don't assume they'll figure it out. Walk them through everything
  • Send them off with a reason to come back next time they're in town
03

Event guests

Turn a one-time visit into a lasting relationship.

  • Review the BEO ahead of time: group size, timeline, special requests and point of contact, and brief your team
  • Greet and orient them right away: where they are, what's available, how the event will run
  • Welcome them as individuals, not just as part of a group
  • Look for moments to share programming, memberships and upcoming events
  • If someone is having a great time, find out what they'd come back for
  • Have programming cards and membership info on hand
04

Programming guests

On-court and off-court. Guide them in.

  • Know the day's program and activation schedule: format, length, guest count, what's included, what to bring
  • Confirm the instructor or host is set up and ready, and the host desk knows what's running
  • Greet them at the host desk, by name if possible
  • Explain how the program works: what to expect, how long, what's provided
  • Walk them to the court or activation space. Don't just point
  • Solo? Introduce them to the group or instructor before you leave
  • Check in midway through
05

Members

They've invested in Rally. Honor that every visit.

  • Know their names, preferences, usual order and schedule
  • Greet them like you've been expecting them
  • A simple “great to see you again” goes further than you think
  • They should hear about new programming and events first
  • When something goes wrong for a member, move faster and care more
Consistency is everything with members. They've been here enough to know what great looks like, and they notice when the standard slips more than any other guest.
