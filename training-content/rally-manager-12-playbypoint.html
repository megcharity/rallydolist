PlayByPoint

PlayByPoint runs our courts: reservations, check-in, programs and the court schedule. Managers need to handle everything the front desk does, plus the tools only managers can use. Start with PlayByPoint's own short training courses, then practice the Rally-specific parts with your trainer.

The goal

Guests should never feel the software. Every booking, check-in and change should be smooth enough that all they notice is the welcome.

Start with the Academy

PlayByPoint has free, on-demand courses for each role. They're mobile-friendly and take about 10–15 minutes each. Complete both before your first shift running the front desk.

Operator / Front Desk course

Daily counter work.

  • Checking in players
  • Managing court reservations
  • Handling drop-ins and walk-ons
  • Accepting payments and viewing bookings
  • Running reports and viewing player activity

Manager course

Configuring the club.

  • Facility details, staff accounts, schedules and court availability
  • Memberships and player access
  • Reservation, lesson, pricing and program rules
  • Communication tools, announcements and promotions
  • Reports and support resources

Find the courses: PlayByPoint Academy · help.playbypoint.com

Who can do what

Your PlayByPoint role decides what you can change.

Operator · front desk
  • Daily tasks and helping players with bookings
  • Posts, widgets, leaderboards and banner ads
  • Can't edit facility, booking, payment or membership rules
  • Some settings may be visible, but saving can be blocked
Manager
  • Full facility-level control
  • Booking, pricing, cancellation and membership rules
  • Courts, schedules, programs and staff roles
  • Payments, card readers and hardware
  • Audit logs

Blocking a court

For maintenance, events or anything that takes a court out of play.

  1. Open the calendarAdmin dashboard → Calendar. Click an open slot on the court → Add Booking.
  2. Pick the typeChoose RESERVATION, LESSON, MAINTENANCE or OTHER, then pick or type a Type.
  3. Set courts and datesCheck SCHEDULE RECURRING EVENT, select each COURT, set DATE END and REPEAT ON. Same-day block: end date = today, no days checked.
  4. Save and confirmClick Book, then check the calendar shows the court as unavailable. To change or remove it, click the block → edit or Delete.

Court status

A message at the top of our website and app that tells players about conditions. It's informational only: it doesn't close courts or block reservations.

StatusUse it when
Courts wet – check back soonCourts are wet and not yet playable
Lightning in area – play delayedStorms are nearby and play is paused
Event in progress – limited courts availableAn event is using some courts
All courts playableThe default, when nothing else applies
Custom StatusAnything else, in your own words
Weather changes, status changes. Update it from the admin dashboard as often as needed. Because it doesn't block bookings, if courts truly can't be used, block them too.

Audit logs

Managers can see who changed what.

  • Settings → General → Audit Logs
  • Each entry shows when, what area, which player or object, the action, the manager, and the before and after values
  • Filter by category, date range or user
  • Currently tracks player profile updates and reservation pricing changes
