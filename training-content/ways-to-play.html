Skill Levels

Every program is matched to skill levels. If a guest isn't sure where they fit, these quick descriptions help. Don't stress about getting it exact. Our pros sort out levels on the court.

  • NewbieNever played before.
  • Level 1BeginnerNew to the game. Learning basic rules and strokes.
  • Level 2Beginner+Building control, knows how to score, can keep short rallies going.
  • Level 3IntermediateFairly consistent. Can place serves and returns.
  • Level 4Intermediate+Better directional control, solid third-shot drops, knows some strategy.
  • Level 5AdvancedPlays offense and defense with power and precision.
  • Level 6EliteMastered strategy. Reads opponents' play.

Leagues

Best for guests who want recurring sessions.

Same crew, every week, for a whole season. Leagues run from Level 1 to advanced, with options that are more social or more competitive.

Every week, all season

Every league includes

  • A 7-week season that ends with a tournament in week 7, based on standings.
  • The end-of-season party for all leagues.
  • 15% off food and non-alcoholic drinks.
  • Easy sign-up. Team leagues let guests sign up solo, with 2–3 friends, or as a full team of 4. We fill in the rest.

Our Clubs

Two weekly club leagues, each with its own vibe and perks. Club members will ask you for their drinks, so this part matters most at the bar.

Hot Girls Play Pickleball

Who
Women of Charlotte who love competition and connection. All levels welcome.
Format
7-week season. Solo sign-up. Rotating play with an individual leaderboard. Ends with a tournament in week 7.
Perks
Weekly social, custom merch, 5 punch-ticket items (usually smoothies), a secret menu item, 15% off food and non-alcoholic drinks.

Just Dudes

Who
Charlotte guys who like friendly rivalry and making new friends.

7-week season. Sign up as a pair or free agent. Grouped by skill from week 2. Ends with a playoff tournament.
Perks
Weekly social, pre-match hangs, custom hat, 5 club drinks (punch card), a secret drink menu, 15% off food and non-alcoholic drinks.

Club members redeem their included items with a punch ticket or card. See Included Drinks below for how to ring them in.

Pop-up programs too

Rally Social and Taste of Pickleball always include a drink, but we often add one to pop-up programs as well. Whenever you see a new program on the schedule, ask your manager if it includes a drink.

  • Finding the $0 items in Toast: every included drink has its own $0 item in Toast. Ask your manager to walk you through where they are.
  • No ticket, punch, or card? The drink is full price.
  • Secret menu items are always full price, even for club members.
  • Out of punches? Extra drinks are full price.
  • Not sure? Check the bar cheat sheet or ask your manager.

The One Rule

If you remember one thing from this training, make it this.

15% off

All classes, socials, and club members get 15% off food and non-alcoholic drinks.

  • Alcohol is always full price. Never apply the 15% to alcohol.
  • Free drinks are separate. Included drinks rung in at $0 (see above) are the only comped drinks. Never comp anything beyond that.
