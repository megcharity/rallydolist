Manager Training
Checklist

Everything a new Rally manager or team lead needs to walk through with the manager they report to. Each item points to where it's covered. Items marked Cover in person don't have an SOP yet, so talk them through face to face.

The goal

Cover every topic with your trainer until you can run it on your own, with confidence, on any shift.

1 · Learn it

Read the page or SOP the item points to, or set a time to cover it in person.

2 · Review it

Walk through it together with your trainer. Check Reviewed.

3 · Do it

Once you've done it on a real shift on your own, your trainer checks Can do it.
