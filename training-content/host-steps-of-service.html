Step 1

Greet the Guest

Every visit starts the moment the door opens. Acknowledge the guest right away, then find out why they're here. That answer decides everything that happens next.

    Give a verbal acknowledgement the moment the guest walks through the door, with a smile.

    "Welcome to Rally. I can check you in here."

Your resources

Keep both of these at the host desk:

  • Floor chart shows which Captain is assigned to each court and section.
  • Programming pamphlet for questions about leagues, Courts & Coffee, Social and other programs.

Always escort

Whenever possible, walk the guest to where they're going: their table, the bar, the cafe or their court. A warm handoff creates a better experience than pointing across the room.

Step 2A & 2B

Dining Guests

Guests here for food and drinks, not to play. What you offer depends on the time of day: the cafe in the morning and at lunch, the full-service restaurant and bar at dinner.

  1. A

    Cafe, breakfast & lunch

    Tap to see the steps

    Cafe, breakfast & lunch

    Ask if they'd like their order to go or to sit down.

    "Will you be grabbing something to go, or would you like to sit down and eat with us?"

    To go
    • Walk them halfway toward the cafe.
    • Point out the barista and cafe counter. Let them know they'll order directly from the barista.
    • Tell them what's offered: coffees, matchas, smoothies and breakfast items.
    • Returning guests who know the way: radio the barista that a person or group is headed their way.
    Sit down
    • Seat the guest at a table.
    • Bring menus and water.
    • Radio the barista or Captain to let them know a guest has been seated.

    To go: "Our barista is right over there — they can get you set up with coffee, matcha, a smoothie, or something from our breakfast menu."

    Sit down: "Here's some water and menus to start — [barista's name] will be right over to take your order."

    Tap to flip back
  2. B

    Dinner & bar

    Tap to see the steps

    Dinner & bar

    Ask if they'd like to sit at the bar or at a table.

    "Would you like to sit at the bar, or would you prefer a table?"

    Bar
    • Walk them to the bar.
    • Introduce them to the bartender by name.
    Table
    • Seat them, and bring water and menus.
    • Check the floor chart, then radio the Captain assigned to that section that guests have been seated.

    Bar: "This is [bartender's name] — they'll be taking great care of you at the bar tonight."

    Table: "Here's some water and menus to start — I'll let your server know you're all set."

    Do they have a reservation?

    Do they have a reservation?

    Yes, booked
    1. Check them in.
    2. Add the guests on the reservation, and make sure waivers are signed.

    No, walk-in

  4. 4

    Checking in a program or league player

    Tap to see the steps

    Checking in a program or league player

    • Know our offerings. Classes (Rally Rookies for beginners, Pickleball Pump) and leagues (Leisure League, Rally Ranks) — know which one they're here for and what it's for.
    • Tell them exactly where to go — which court or area, and when it starts.
    • Check if their ticket includes a drink and tell them what it covers before they ask.
    • Set expectations: how long it runs, what to bring, and what happens when it ends.
    • Never just check them in — walk them through all of it and make them feel welcome.

    "You're all set for Rally Rookies on Court 3 — it starts at 10 and runs about an hour. Your ticket includes a drink from the cafe or bar, so grab one before or after. Let me know if you have any questions!"

    Tap to flip back
  5. 5
    Selling starts here

    Share key information and our offering

    Tap to see the steps
    Selling starts here

    Share key information and our offering

    Start mentioning what we offer as early as possible. You're setting your Captain up to sell, and setting the guest's expectation that food and drinks are part of the Rally experience, not an afterthought.

    • Lay of the land: restrooms here, cafe there, bar and restaurant over there. Skip this for regulars.
    • Our offering: tell them their Captain will bring food and drinks right to their court, and call out something specific, like a crushable drink or a court snack you love. Do this for regulars too.
    • Current specials: let every guest know about anything we're running right now.

    "While you play, your Captain can bring drinks and snacks right to your court. If you're thirsty after warm-ups, the [drink] is my go-to."

    Keep it going on the walk to the court (step 2 of arrival timing), then finish with the handoff script that names your favorite item.

Step 2C.2

Arrival Timing

They're checked in. Now what? It depends on when they arrived compared to their reservation start time.

A

Early, court open, everyone here

  • Let them know you can get them on court early.
  • Take them to the court right away.
  • Adjust RallyTrack so the tablet is ready.
  • Move reservations around if needed to get them on early.
B

Early, still waiting on guests

  • Let them know you'll take them to the court once everyone arrives.
  • Recommend they head to the bar or cafe for a drink while they wait.
C

Early, court still occupied

  • Ask them to check back 2 minutes before their start time.
  • Recommend they head to the cafe or bar while they wait.
D

On time

  • Great! Let them know you're ready for them and will take them to the court now.

Anyone waiting (B and C): send them to the cafe or bar to relax and enjoy the club.
