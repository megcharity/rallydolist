Side Work
in the Cafe

A stocked, clean, dialed-in cafe is what lets you focus on guests instead of scrambling. This chapter walks you through how we open and close the cafe, and why each step matters. The printed checklist lives in the cafe; this is where you learn it.

The Rally Standard

Know every task and the standard it has to meet, then exceed it. If you're ever unsure about a task, ask the MOD. We're here to help.

Set up the next person

The closer sets up the opener. The opener sets up service. Every task on the checklist is a favor to whoever is behind the counter next, so leave it better than you found it, and initial each section when it's done.

How a Shift Flows

Cafe side work happens in four parts. Here's what each part is for and every task it covers.

1

First thing in

Goal: start the things that take time to heat up.

  • Run the rinse cycle on the coffee urn.
  • Turn on the oven.
2

Opening

Goal: the cafe is ready before the first guest walks up.

  • Condiment bar: restock sugars, stir sticks and drink stoppers
  • Espresso bar: pull out syrups and put pour spouts on (note any that need to be made), put scale chargers away, fill hoppers with beans, dial in espresso, put rags out, fill the spoon cup with water, stock cups, lids and sleeves
  • Pastry case: put pastries in the case and make sure the signs look good
  • Brew bar: weigh out and grind beans, brew Rally Blend, fill the ice bin
  • Front of house: tidy retail bags and coffee signs at the register, put away the "Cafe Closed" signs, put out cornhole boards and bags
3

Closing: espresso & brew bar

Goal: clean equipment and a fully stocked bar for the opener.

  • Wash: steaming pitchers, stir spoons, stainless steel cups, drip mats, matcha/beet scoops and shot glasses
  • Restock: milk (make half and half and sweet cream if needed), syrups and tea concentrates, tea shelf, to-go cups and lids, sleeves, straws and napkins, condiment bar
  • Fridges: wipe them out
  • Syrups: wash spouts, wipe bottles, put bottles away
  • Espresso machine: back flush each grouphead with Cafiza and clean around the gaskets, soak and scrub portafilters (not the rubber part), wipe down the machine and stainless, clean the pitcher rinser
  • Grinders: wipe the hopper and espresso grinder, clean up loose grounds
  • Brewed coffee: dump leftover coffee, wipe the urn, run a rinse cycle on the coffee maker
  • Kegerator: wipe with a microfiber rag
4

Closing: pastry case & general

Goal: the opener walks into a clean, reset cafe.

  • Pastry case: put leftover pastries in a container in the fridge (2:45pm), wash plates and tongs, restock bags and wax bags, wipe the inside and top of the case
  • Oven: cool it down (2:30pm), then clean the oven and trays
  • Ice well: pour hot water down until melted
  • Dishes: all clean and put away
  • Counters: sanitize, including the condiment bar and the side counter under the window
  • Floors: sweep and mop (Monday and Tuesday only)
  • Finish: put dirty rags away, take out all cafe trash and re-line the cans

Words to Know

Terms you'll see on the checklist and hear behind the counter.

Dialing inAdjusting the grind each morning until espresso shots taste right. Bitter usually means grind coarser; sour usually means grind finer.
HopperThe container on top of the grinder that holds whole beans. Fill it at open; wipe it at close.
Rally BlendOur brewed coffee. Weigh and grind the beans fresh before brewing.
CafizaThe espresso machine cleaner used to back flush groupheads and soak portafilters.
Back flushingRunning Cafiza through each grouphead to clean out coffee oils, then cleaning around the gaskets.
PortafilterThe handle that holds the coffee basket. Soak and scrub it at close, but not the rubber part.
Pitcher rinserThe rinser for steaming pitchers. Leftover Cafiza water goes down it at close, then clean it.
RinseThe cleaner used for the brewed coffee maker and steam wands on deep-clean days.
