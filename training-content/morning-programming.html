Morning
Programming

Many of our morning guests are here for a program, and some programs include a drink with the ticket. Today you'll learn which drinks are included, how to ring them in on Toast, and why the players behind those tickets are some of the most important guests you'll serve.

The Rally Standard

Every program drink gets rung in, and every program player gets known by name.

Connection is key

Our morning program players are our regulars. Building relationships with them builds community at Rally, and it builds the kind of relationships that lead to better tips. Learn their names and their usual orders, ask how their games went, and greet them like the regulars they are.

What you'll explore today

Courts & Coffee

  • Our most popular morning program
  • Every player receives a drink ticket
  • The ticket gets them a free cold brew, drip coffee or tea
  • Ring it in as Courts & Coffee on Toast

Other morning programs

  • Players also receive free drinks
  • They show a punch card or ticket
  • Ring the drink in at $0

Ringing in & tips

  • When a drink is rung in at $0, a percentage of the ticket sale goes toward tips
  • A program drink that isn't rung in leaves that tip share out
  • Always ring it in, even when the guest isn't paying

Ringing In a Program Drink

When a program player comes to the counter with a ticket or punch card, follow these steps.

  1. Check for the ticket or punch cardCourts & Coffee players bring a drink ticket. Players in other morning programs show a punch card or ticket.
  2. Ring it in on ToastCourts & Coffee: ring it in as Courts & Coffee, for a cold brew, drip coffee or tea. Other morning programs: ring the drink in at $0.
  3. Make it and connectMake the drink to standard and call their name. Ask how their game went, and remember their order for next time.
Have a manager show you how. Before you ring in a Courts & Coffee ticket or a $0 program drink on your own, ask a manager to walk you through it on Toast.
