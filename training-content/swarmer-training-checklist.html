Swarmer Training
Checklist

Everything a Rally swarmer needs to know, in one checklist. Swarmers run and buss across the whole venue, and they're hospitality people first. Sit down with your manager and work through it together. When every item is reviewed and you can do it on your own, you're ready for the floor.

The goal

Review every item with a manager until you know how to do all of it, on your own, with confidence.

1 · Review it

Your manager walks you through the item: tells you, shows you, then has you try it. Check Reviewed.

2 · Do it

Once you can do it on your own, your manager checks Can do it.
