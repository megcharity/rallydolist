Reminders

Still part of the job, just not the headline six.

  • Acknowledge every guest.Make eye contact and smile as you pass them.
  • Ask every person in the group individually.Don't assume based on one person's answer.
  • Bringing more than two drinks? A tray is mandatory.
  • Every court-reservation tab needs a card on file.Closed out 5 minutes before the reservation ends.
  • Keep the lounges and tables tidy.
  • Make sure courts and tables have napkins and utensils.
  • Never ask "Are you good?"Offer something specific instead — "Can I get you a beer, a smoothie, or something else to drink?"
  • Respond to hands when called, and to the Host's court radio time-calls.
