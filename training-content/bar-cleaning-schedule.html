Bar Cleaning
Schedule

A clean bar is a fast bar. On top of your opening and closing side work, the bar has cleaning tasks that happen every day, on set days of the week, and on the 1st of every month. The printed schedule lives at the bar; this is where you learn it.

The Rally Standard

Leave the bar cleaner than you found it. If you're ever unsure about a task, ask the MOD.

Every day

Done every shift.

  • Wipe all stainless steel
  • Wash the soda spouts
  • Wipe the draft handles
  • Wipe down the door
  • Wash the rubber pour caps
  • Space out the back bar liquor
  • Polish glasses, and look for cracks and chips

Every week

Each day has its own task. Initial the printed sheet when it's done.

Monday

  • Wipe inside the coolers
  • Pull out the coolers and wipe the tops and the windows

Tuesday

  • Organize the walk-in
  • Mop the walk-in
  • Batch syrups

Wednesday

  • Clean and dust the liquor shelves
  • Clean the floor drains

Thursday

  • Clean and sanitize ALL stainless steel racks
  • Wipe and organize the register area
  • Dump the broken glass bucket

Every month

On the 1st of the month.

  • Organize the bottom shelves
  • Wash and wipe the back bar shelf mats
  • Dust the light fixtures
  • Glassware inventory
