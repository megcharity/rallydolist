Training SOP

Oct 14, 2026 · rewritten Oct 21, 2026 to match the current Training tab

Every new hourly associate finishes training in 5 days, and every manager can see where each one stands in the Training tab. This is for GMs and managers who get a new hire ready, schedule it, and sign it off.

What "done" meansAll 5 days signed off, the position test passed (12 of 15 or better), the bingo card turned in, the position checklist signed, and a manager check-in completed. Signing off Day 5 marks the last three automatically.

Before you schedule training

Day 1 can't happen until all four of these are done.

Scheduling the training week

  1. Pick 5 shifts in HotSchedules

    Matched to real shifts for the new hire's own position — they don't need to be consecutive days.

  2. Schedule each shift as FOH Training
  3. Write the training day in the shift notes

    "Day 1 training" on the first shift, through "Day 5 training" on the last — so anyone glancing at the schedule knows exactly where that associate is.

  4. Cover the cross-shadow positions

    Make sure someone in each of the other positions is also working Days 2–4, so the hour of cross-shadowing has someone to shadow.

  5. Name a trainer for each day in the shift notes

    Their own-position trainer, and whoever's covering the cross-shadow position that hour.

  6. Get them on as many events as you can

    If none come up during the week, walk them through event setup anyway. Either way, never let them work an event alone before they've shadowed a few first.

The 5-day plan

Every hourly position works their own position first, every day — that's the main part of every shift. Cross-training happens as a single 1-hour add-on, one different position each day.

Day 1: get them started

  1. Sit down with the associate

    Before or at the start of their shift. Walk them through how the 5 days work — online reading plus floor time every day, a daily sign-off, a test and the bingo card on Day 5, and what "done" means.

  2. Add them as a trainee

    Training tile → Training PIN → + Add Trainee → name + position → Create. No manager PIN needed for this step.

  3. Have them tap their own name

    It opens a welcome screen recapping the 5 days, then drops them into Day 1's reading and floor tasks.

  4. Hand them the training checklist

    The bingo card is digital now and lives on their own Day 1 — nothing to hand over, though you can still print one from Resources for a physical copy on the floor.

  5. Last online item today: their bio

    Five quick questions (hometown, go-to order, a fun fact, what they're excited about, what they do for fun) — the page explains why: it's how we introduce them to the team on GroupMe. Grab their answers once they're done and post the welcome.

  6. Sign off Day 1 at the end of the shift

    Same process as every day — see below.

GroupMe intro example. "👋 Everyone say hi to [Name], joining us as a [Position]! They're from [hometown], their go-to order is [X], and a fun fact: [Y]. They're most excited about [Z] here at Rally. Give them a warm welcome when you see them on the floor!"

Day 5: the test and sign-off

Day 5 is different: it's the last shift, and signing it off closes out training completely.

  1. Associate takes both tests

    The position test and the Rally Menu Test, on the tablet.

  2. Associate comes to you with their results

    Position test score, Rally Menu Test answers, and their completed bingo card — open it on the tablet, it's theirs, tracked automatically as they tap squares through the week.

  3. Go over any missed test questions together
  4. Tap "Training Completed"

    Enter the manager PIN and your name. This one action signs off Day 5 and marks the bingo card, training checklist, and manager check-in all done — no need to find and check three more boxes.

  5. They're done

    Congratulations screen for them, drops off the kiosk trainee picker, shows as Complete on your dashboard.

Under 12 on the test. Review the missed questions together (shown right on the results screen), then retake from the same button. Every attempt is saved, and the best score counts.

Dashboard and weekly review

Manager → Training shows everyone currently training: days done, test score, bingo, checklist, check-in and a status.

In progressStill working through their 5 days. No automatic "behind" flag — judge pace from start date and days signed off.
Retest neededBest score under 12. Book a review and a retake.
CompleteAll five conditions met — happens automatically on Day 5 sign-off. Drops off the kiosk picker too.
ArchivedThe person left before finishing.

Each week, the GM or training lead:

  • Opens the Training tab and checks every "Retest needed" associate, and eyeballs anyone who seems stalled
  • Confirms next week's new hires have all 5 shifts scheduled as FOH Training, with shift notes marking the training day
  • Archives anyone who left before finishing

Training tablet & resources

Keep one tablet (or more, for multiple training spots) in kiosk mode, Rally Do List open to the role screen. Training only happens on the tablet — not on anyone's personal phone. The shared Training PIN gets anyone into the trainee picker; creating a trainee and reading/taking tests don't need more than that. Signing off a day, and any destructive action, needs the Manager PIN — that's the real security boundary.

Manager → Training → 🖨 Resources has every print-ready page in one place: training checklists, side work sheets, the bar cleaning schedule, R&D policy, and more, grouped by position. Each opens in a new tab — print or save as PDF from your browser.

Troubleshooting

A day was signed off by mistake

Manager → Training, expand the trainee's card, tap Undo next to that day.

Added a duplicate or test trainee by accident

Expand their card and tap 🗑 Delete, or tap the 🗑 on their kiosk card directly (asks for the Manager PIN first). Deleting is permanent.

A trainee doesn't show up on the kiosk anymore

They've likely hit Complete — check Manager → Training; they'll still be there. If archived by mistake, tap 📦 Archived (N) at the top, find them, and tap 📦↩ to restore.

No event happened during the training week

Initial the event squares once the associate does them on a later shift, then mark the bingo card turned in at Day 5.

An associate left before finishing

Open their record and tap Archive.

Baristas and the bingo card

Baristas don't shadow the bartender or swarmer, so 2 squares can't be completed. Expected — note it and move on, it doesn't block sign-off.

Swarmers and the bingo card

Swarmers don't ring anything in on Toast, so their card automatically swaps the 4 sales/ring-in squares for squares that match their actual job. Nothing for you to do — it's built in.
