Managing
the PULSE

PULSE is how we manage the energy and environment of Rally. Managers need to know the specific standard for each component and get into the habit of checking and adjusting all shift, not just at open.

The Rally Standard

The room is always communicating something. Our job is to make sure it’s always working for us, not against us.

Five components. One standard.

Rally is warm, inviting, premium and effortlessly cool, and the space has to reflect that at every hour. Get these right and guests linger longer, spend more and come back more often.

P · Playlists

Music and volume should be intentional, not incidental. Music should always feel like it was chosen, not defaulted to.

  • Only Rally-approved playlists. No personal playlists, no algorithm-generated stations
  • Adjust volume when the venue is particularly busy or empty
SpaceMorning 7–11amMid-day 12–4pmEvening 4pm–close
Outdoor courts & lounges30–4030–4045–50
Indoor courts25–3025–3035–40
Other spaces2525–3035–40
  • Indoor courts: lower end when courts are empty, higher end when players are active
  • Other spaces: lower end when the venue is quieter, higher end as energy builds
  • Evenings should feel like a lively social venue: not a nightclub, not a library

U · Upkeep

Upkeep is the foundation everything else sits on. A beautifully lit, perfectly soundtracked room still falls flat if the tables are sticky.

Guest-facing areas

  • All surfaces free of food debris and liquid rings, including the café and condiment table
  • Bar stools tucked in and aligned at a slight angle from the bar
  • Chairs straight and tucked into tables
  • Table signage in place and presentable
  • Umbrellas open and positioned correctly (weather permitting)

Courts & outdoor

  • All courts clean: no stray balls, barriers straight
  • All outdoor tables, chairs, benches and bar wiped clean of dust and pollen

Back of house

  • Captains' quarters, manager offices and BOH areas clean and sanitary
  • Supplies, radios and equipment out of guest sight when open

L · Lighting

Bright and open during the day, warm and intimate as the evening builds. The venue should always feel moody and intentional, never overly bright.

Time of daySceneNotes
DaytimeScene 4Bright, open, energized
Evening / nightScene 3Warmer and dimmer. The dimmer the better as the night goes on
Transition gradually. Move from Scene 4 to Scene 3 as the evening builds. Don't wait until it's fully dark, and when in doubt, err on the side of dimmer. Adjust the ping pong wing lighting to match activity and the mood of the rest of the venue.

S · Screens

Every screen either reinforces the Rally brand or undercuts it. Default settings matter.

ScreenDefault contentNotes
Player's Lounge TVsCurrent promo graphic, or the Rally logo screen if there's no promo—
All other TVsAlternate: promo graphic on one, Rally logo screen on the nextLive sports on request only
Courtside RallyTrack displaysOn and showing the correct screenConfirm guest names are showing correctly
No news. No talk shows. No sports highlights. They pull guests into passive watching instead of engaging with the space and each other.
TV check
  • All TVs on and working at open. Flag any that are off or showing errors
  • RallyTrack courtside displays on and showing correct information
  • Content updated when new promo graphics are available

E · Environment

Physical comfort matters as much as how the space looks and sounds. Open garage doors create an indoor-outdoor feel in the Main House.

Garage doors
  • Below 50°F: keep doors closed
  • 55–60°F: open all doors
  • 60–70°F: one to three doors. Use judgment
  • 80°F+: keep doors closed
Main House temperature
  • Winter: 60–62°F for comfortable playing
  • Summer: 60–70°F for comfortable playing
Garage doors open = outdoor fans on. Fans are required by health code. This is non-negotiable.

The PULSE check

Run it on every Figure of 8 and walkthrough. It should become a trained instinct, not a formal checklist.

  1. P · PlaylistsIs the music right? Volume correct for the time of day and the energy in the room? Rally-approved playlist playing?
  2. U · UpkeepIs every guest-facing area clean, tidy and reset? Stools aligned, tables cleared, outdoor areas wiped, courts clean?
  3. L · LightingIs the lighting scene correct? Transitioning as the evening builds? Ping pong wing adjusted?
  4. S · ScreensAre all TVs on and showing the right content? No news, no highlights. RallyTrack displays on and correct?
  5. E · EnvironmentIs the temperature comfortable? Garage doors right for the weather? Fans on if the doors are open?
PULSE isn't a one-time opening task. It's a continuous standard, all shift long.
