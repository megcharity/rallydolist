Private Events

Rally prides itself on its events. We host everything from birthdays to corporate outings and tournaments right here on our courts. Know our event packages inside and out, and bring the same service and hospitality to every event that we bring to every guest.

Events are also one of the best ways to make great tips. Guests pay a lot for these parties, and it's our job to deliver real hospitality. Great service brings them back, and repeat business means more events and more tips.

Ringing in event orders

How you ring it in keeps the bill and our inventory right.

Each event is assigned its own code number. Captains should clock into that code whenever they're ringing in replens or drinks for the event — it's how Rally tracks inventory and calculates and keeps track of additional sales as they relate to events.

  1. Clock in to the event code on Toast before you ring anything for the event.
  2. Taking an order from a walk-in guest? Switch the server back to yourself first.
  3. Ring every drink in, even ones the group has already paid for (see below).

Package or consumption?

  • Beverage package: ring included drinks at $0. They already paid. Ringing it in tracks inventory and what we gave out, without charging twice.
  • Beverage on consumption: ring drinks at full retail price. This counts toward the group's minimum, or it's additional sales.
  • Not included in their package? Ring at retail and capture a card for it.

Setting up the food station

Captains set up the station for each item in the group's food package.

  1. Collect the staff BEO from the events office before the event. Smaller events run off the BEO; larger events also have a battle plan, which the MOD walks through at the start and uses to assign tasks.
  2. At the end of the event, fill out the event sheet and put it back in the event folder with the BEO.
  3. Set out equipment for each dish: heat lamps for hot handheld food like sliders, chafers to keep buffet-style food warm.
  4. Give every dish its own serving utensil. No exceptions.
  5. Put out plates, napkins, silverware if needed, and food labels.

Chafers: add about ½ inch of water and light the sternos about 5 minutes before food arrives. Confirm chafer count with the MOD or kitchen.

Running the station

The captain owns the food station for the whole event.

  • Wipe it down constantly.
  • Watch for food on the floor and clean it up right away.
  • Restock plates and cutlery before they run out.
  • Replenish food on time, following the BEO or battle plan.

Guests should never be waiting on their food.
