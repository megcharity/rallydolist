Bourbon · served up

Quick check

Answer out loud, then tap to reveal
1A guest loves espresso martinis. What do you suggest?

The Usual Suspect. Vodka, Kahlúa and Grind with salted caramel cream. Mention the dairy and caffeine.

2Which new seasonal has egg white?

Purple Fog. On the Zero Proof side, the Raspberry Beret has egg white too.

3Name the two bourbon seasonals.

Flight Risk (spirit-forward, served up) and the Salted Honey Hibiscus Smash (tart, pink, on the rocks).

4A guest wants a dessert-y drink. Which do you recommend?

Boozy Root Beer Float, The Usual Suspect or Sorbet Soiree. The float and The Usual Suspect both have dairy, so mention it.

5A guest loves margaritas. Which do you recommend?

Pear Necessities, our fall margarita with reposado tequila, lime, pear and maple. If they like heat, the Spicy Tommy's is still on the menu.

6A guest wants something light and bubbly that isn't a glass of prosecco.

Hugo's Figgy Spritz. Lillet Blanc, elderflower, fig, prosecco and soda.

7A guest likes Old Fashioneds or spirit-forward drinks. Which do you recommend?

Flight Risk, our play on a Paper Plane with bourbon, Aperol, amaro and spiced apple cider. The Rally Old Fashioned on our signatures list is another good pick.

8A guest wants something hoppy. What do you suggest?

Juicy Jay (juicy, tropical) or Surfax on draft, or Pernicious IPA in a can. For something less bitter, try the Sugar Creek Pale Ale.

9A guest wants something light. What do you suggest?

Rewind Lager or Town's Spratt's on draft. In cans: Michelob Ultra, Miller Lite or Montucky.

10A guest wants something fruity. What do you suggest?

Broken Tarted (tart sour), Mango Cart (mango wheat), Bold Rock Apple cider or Ginger's Revenge.

11A guest wants something darker or malty. What do you suggest?

Copper on draft (bready, toffee and caramel) or Guinness in a can.

12A guest isn't drinking but still wants a beer. What do you suggest?

Best Day Kolsch, Run Wild IPA or Guinness Zero.

Before you hit the floor
  • I can describe all 10 seasonals in one line each.
  • I can recognize each drink at the pass by its glass and garnish.
  • I know which drinks have egg white, dairy, coffee or heat.
  • I tasted at least two of the new drinks, or know which ones to taste next.
