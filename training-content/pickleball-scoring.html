Pickleball Scoring

There are two ways to keep score: side out scoring (the classic way) and rally scoring (the faster way). Either way, a rally ends when someone hits it out, into the net, or lets it bounce twice. The only question is whether that rally earns a point.

How to call the score in doubles

4Serving team
2Other team
1Server #1 or #2

The server says it out loud before every serve.

Side Out Scoring

Only the serving team can score.

Games to 11

The rules

  • Win the rally while serving? Your team gets a point. The same player serves again, from the other side of the court.
  • Win the rally while receiving? No point. You just win the chance to serve.
  • Both partners serve. When your first server loses a rally, the partner serves next (that's server #2). When server #2 loses a rally, the serve goes to the other team. That's called a "side out."
  • The start is special. The first team to serve only gets one server, so the game begins with the call 0 – 0 – 2.
  • Say three numbers. Your score, their score, then your server number (1 or 2). In singles, just say the two scores.
  • Games go to 11. You must win by 2 (so 11–9 wins, but 11–10 keeps going).

Quick example

Score callWhat happens
0 – 0 – 2Team A serves first and wins the rally. Point for A.
1 – 0 – 2Team A serves again and loses the rally. No point. Side out.
0 – 1 – 1Team B's first server serves and loses. No point. Their partner serves next.
0 – 1 – 2Team B's second server serves and wins. Point for B.
1 – 1 – 2Team B serves again…

Tip: After every side out, the player standing on the right side serves first.

Rally Scoring

Every rally is a point, no matter who served.

Games to 15 or 21

The rules

  • Win the rally while serving? Your team gets a point. The server switches sides and serves again.
  • Win the rally while receiving? Your team gets a point and the serve.
  • Only one server per turn. There is no server #2. Lose the rally and the serve goes straight to the other team.
  • Say two numbers. The serving team's score first, then the other team's score.
  • Who serves? If your team's score is even, the player on the right serves. If it's odd, the player on the left serves.
  • Games usually go to 15 or 21. You still must win by 2. Agree on the number before you start.
  • Game point can be won by either team. Under the 2026 rules, you don't have to be serving to win the last point.

Quick example

Score callWhat happens
0 – 0Team A serves and wins the rally. Point for A.
1 – 0Team A serves again and loses the rally. Point for B, and B gets the serve.
1 – 1Team B serves (score is odd, so the player on the left serves) and loses. Point for A, and A serves.
2 – 1Team A serves…
