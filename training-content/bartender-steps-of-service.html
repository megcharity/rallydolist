Steps 1–2

Greet & Take the Order

From the moment a guest sits down to the moment you start making their drink.

The bar builds regulars. A genuine recommendation and a few seconds of real conversation is what turns a one-time guest into a regular. Getting the drink right is technical skill — getting them to come back is hospitality.

A guest's visit at the bar

1 · Greet
Eye contact and a warm hello, right away
2 · Order
Menu, specials, and what you're loving right now
3 · Make
Recipe, glassware, garnish, quality check
4 · Check back
Confirm it's right, offer another before they're empty
5 · Close
Confirm payment, thank them by name

    Acknowledge & greet

    • Make eye contact and give a warm greeting the moment a guest sits at the bar, even if you're mixing another order.
    • If you're slammed, acknowledge them immediately, then follow up properly as soon as you can.

    "Hey, I'll be with you in just a second!"

    Where relationships start

    Take the order

    This is your real chance to connect, not just take an order. Guests remember the bartender who made them feel looked after — that's how regulars are made.

    • Walk every guest through the menu — beer, wine and cocktails — even if they seem to know what they want. Mention that our syrups are made in house. Non-negotiable
    • Tell them what you're personally loving right now. A genuine, specific recommendation builds more trust than a generic suggestion.
    • Tell them about any specials we're running.
    • If they're still undecided, suggest something specific rather than asking "What can I get you?"

    "Honestly, I've been loving the Rally Paloma lately — nice citrus kick. Want me to make you one?"

Steps 3–4

Make, Serve & Check Back

Every drink goes out to standard, and the next drink is offered before the guest has to ask.

  1. 3

    Make & serve

    • Make the drink to standard and recipe, with a jigger for every pour.
    • Serve it in the right glassware with the right garnish.
    • Quality-check it before you hand it over.

  2. 4

    Check back

    • Check back shortly after serving to confirm the drink is right.
    • Offer another when they're close to empty. Don't wait to be asked.
    • Use the moment to connect — ask how their game went, and remember a name or a usual order for next time.
