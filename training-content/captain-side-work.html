Side Work
on the Floor

Clean lounges, stocked captains' quarters and reset tables are what let you focus on guests instead of scrambling. This chapter walks you through how we open, run and close the floor, and why each step matters.

The Rally Standard

Know every task and the standard it has to meet, then exceed it. If you're ever unsure about a task, ask the MOD. We're here to help.

Set up the next person

The closer sets up the opener. The opener sets up service. Every roll-up, reset and restock is a favor to whoever is on the floor next, so leave it better than you found it.

How a Shift Flows

Captain side work happens in five parts. Here's what each part is for and every task it covers.

1

Opening

Goal: your section is spotless and stocked before the first guest.

  • Detail your section: sweep and wipe down
  • Open the buss cart (flip the bin carts)
  • Double-check captains' quarters are stocked for service: plates, cups, carafes, rolls
  • Check the outside lounges: put out menus, QR codes, water, cups and cold towels
  • Check tables for stickiness and water marks
  • Make sure the lounges are crumb-free
  • Fill up the water in the lounges
  • Wipe down the drink trays
2

Throughout the shift

Goal: every lounge and table looks reset, all shift long.

  • Reset setups and rolls with plates
  • Buss tables and lounges
  • Clean all surfaces, including side tables
  • Reset cups at the lounges, the cafe water station and outside
  • Put the correct F&B menus on all courts
  • Spot sweep if necessary
  • Refill waters and cold towels
  • Help the bar polish glasses
  • Restock plates
3

Side work

Goal: the closers and the next shift never run short.

  • Rollups: fill all captains' quarters bins, and roll extra for the closers (more than just the bin)
  • Roll cold towels (not always daily; may be daily in mid-summer)
  • Clean captains' quarters
4

Pre-close (about 1 hour before close)

Goal: start resetting as sections clear out.

  • Wipe down all surfaces; begin cleaning as sections of the venue clear out
  • Reset cups, menus, plates and rolls in the lounges
  • Clean and reset tables with setups
  • Refill rolls in the pizza kitchen captains' quarters
  • Tidy the C4 captains' quarters
  • Remove outdoor couch cushions once the last reservations leave
  • Straighten pillows on the indoor couches
5

Closing

Goal: the opener walks into a clean, reset venue.

  • Dump waters
  • Take down umbrellas
  • Put chairs up (Wednesday–Sunday)
  • Bring inside the remaining couch cushions, menus, QR codes, water and cups, and everything else from outside
  • Clean the water channel and drain under the cafe water station spouts
  • Polish glassware, silverware and plates, and restock
  • Return and restock check presenters, pens and paper
  • Empty trash
  • Spot-check the floor for debris
  • Close open tabs and print your shift report
~1 hr

Pre-close starts about an hour before close.

Wed–Sun

Put chairs up at close.

Rolls +

Fill every bin, then roll extra for the closers.

Words to Know

Terms you'll see on the checklist and hear on the floor.

Captains' quartersThe captain service stations, stocked with plates, cups, carafes and rolls. There's one in the pizza kitchen and one at C4.
Rolls / rollupsSilverware rolled in a napkin. Fill every bin, and roll extra for the closers.
SetupsA table's full reset: rolls and plates in place, ready for the next guests.
Buss cartThe cart for bussed dishes. Open it at the start of the shift by flipping the bin carts.
LoungesThe seating areas by the courts. Keep them crumb-free, with water and cups reset.
Shift reportPrint it after you close your open tabs. It's the last step of your close.
