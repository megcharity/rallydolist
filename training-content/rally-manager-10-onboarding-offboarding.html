Onboarding &
Offboarding

Following this process in order makes sure every new associate is legally compliant, set up on every platform and ready for day one. When someone leaves, it makes sure their access is removed promptly, property is recovered, and paperwork is filed correctly.

The non-negotiable

No associate begins training until their I-9 has been submitted and verified in iSolve.

Onboarding step by step

Owners are noted on every step. Toast comes before HotSchedules because it needs a few hours to sync.

#TaskOwner
Phase 1 · Offer acceptance & paperwork
1Send the offer letter, including the I-9 document request (use the compliant language in Chapter 9). Documents ideally dropped off before the start date, no later than day oneManager
2Sign the offer letter and email it backAssociate
3Forward the signed offer letter to HRManager
4Send all online onboarding documentsHR
5Drop off I-9 documents (their choice from the USCIS lists)Associate
6Scan the I-9 documents and send copies to HRManager
Phase 2 · I-9 verification & welcome email (iSolve)
1Confirm the associate has finished all online onboarding docs. This is your trigger.HR
2Log into iSolve → Manager ViewManager
3Upload and complete the associate's I-9 information in iSolveManager
4Send a personalized Welcome to Rally email: warm welcome, required uniform, first scheduled day, and HotSchedules activation instructionsManager
5Message HR to confirm the I-9 was submitted, with the submission dateManager
Phase 3 · Toast POS
1Toast back office → Employees → Add New EmployeeManager
2Enter first name, last name, phone number and emailManager
3POS access code = last 4 digits of the associate's phone numberManager
4Assign the correct Job Code for their roleManager
5Assign a Training Code before their first shiftManager
Phase 4 · HotSchedules (after the Toast sync)
1Staff List → search the associate's nameManager
2Dropdown next to their name → Send Welcome EmailManager
3Activate their account and set their own credentialsAssociate
Phase 5 · Tip Haus & GroupMe
1Tip Haus → Settings → Employees → Users → Create → EmployeeManager
2Search the name (they appear after Toast setup) and send the invite so they can see daily tipsManager
3Send the GM link for Tip Haus accessManager
4Add them to all relevant in-venue GroupMe channelsManager
Phase 6 · First day
1Add them to Articulate and send the online training linkManager
2Walk through Toast: logging in with their access code and navigating the POSManager
3Walk through HotSchedules: viewing their schedule, confirming shifts, requesting time offManager

Associate offboarding

For hourly associates. Complete the termination paperwork before removing any system access.

  1. Termination formFill out and submit it. Attach evidence of written warnings or coaching conversations, or of voluntary resignation.
  2. Remove all accessGroupMe channels · Toast (deactivate the access code) · HotSchedules · Tip Haus · company website · Articulate.
