Part 1

The 1-Minute Standard

This applies on every shift, whatever is happening in the building.

The 1-minute standard

Every guest is seated within 1 minute of arrival and escorted to their court or table.

Making this happen is the Manager's responsibility. If a team member isn't immediately available, you do it: take waters and menus to seated guests, or walk guests to their courts yourself.

Event type 3 of 4

Leagues & Programming

Building connections and relationships with our regulars is very important. Whatever your role that night, you're visible.

You're running the program
  • Start the program, then roam.
  • Sitting outside with the league the whole time isn't permitted. Your presence is needed inside, and court touching is still required.
You're MOD, not running it
  • Make sure your laps include the outdoor programming.
  • Pop in and talk to guests. Get to know our regulars.

Leagues that allow you to roam

  • Leisure League (+)
  • Ranks
  • Rookies League
  • Guys & Gals League
  • Just Dudes
