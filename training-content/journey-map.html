The New Hire
Journey

Every new hire at Rally trains in five days. Day 1 is the same foundation for everyone. Days 2 to 4 work their own position for the shift, with one hour each day cross-shadowing a different position. Day 5 is their test, a full shift and sign-off.

Before day 1Hired & set up
  • Offer signed; I-9 submitted and verified in iSolve (no training before this)
  • HotSchedules login
  • All 5 training days scheduled, each with a named trainer
Day 1Welcome + foundation
  • Core training online: Hospitality Playbook, The Rally Way, Guest Recovery, Team Essentials, HotSchedules
  • Tour of Rally
  • Pick up the bingo card
Days 2–4Shadow & learn
  • Online learning at the start of the shift
  • Own position for the shift, then an hour cross-shadowing a different one
  • One "show your trainer" check before leaving
Every daySign off the day
  • Trainer checks off the day on the training tablet
  • Bingo squares initialed as they happen
Day 5Test + full shift
  • Position test (15 Qs, pass 12) + Rally Menu Test
  • Full shift in position
  • Checklist sign-off + manager check-in
AfterOn the team
  • Keeps the FOH Training & Guide as their reference
  • Side work and cleaning checklists every shift
