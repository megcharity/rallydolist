Guiding the
Experience

Today you'll bring together your coffee and menu knowledge and your café skills to guide guests through their visit — whether they're grabbing a coffee before an early game or settling in for the morning — while keeping drinks moving and the line flowing.

The Rally Standard

Great café service isn't choosing between the guest at the counter and the drinks waiting to be made. It's making both feel effortless.

Connection is key

Talk to your guests about pickleball. Playing this morning? In a league? Ask how the season is going or who they're playing next. Learn your regulars' names and orders. That's what makes Rally feel like their place.

Heading to a Court or Pod

During your shift, you take care of every guest in the venue. When café guests head out to a court or the garden pods with a tab open, their check goes with them, and you keep serving them there.

  1. Before they leave the counterAsk where they're headed and whether they have a court reservation. Let them know you'll check in with them at their court or pod.
  2. Close or transfer the checkPaying now? Close it out. Keeping a tab? Transfer it in Toast to their court or pod and make sure every item landed on the correct check.
  3. Keep the details with the checkNote any open orders, milk swaps, allergies or modifications, then check in at their court or pod. If your shift ends while they're still here, hand the check and the details to the captain coming on.
If guests leave without paying or a transfer — or you find an open check in Toast that hasn't been transferred — print the check and tell the MOD right away. A description of the guests helps.
