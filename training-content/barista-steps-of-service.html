Beyond the counter

Tables & Courts

In the morning, you take care of guests at the Garden Pods, the 70s and court reservations. These steps follow the Captain guide, adapted for the cafe.

Courtesy on the floor

  • Never ask "Are you good?" Always offer something specific: "Can I get you an iced latte, a smoothie, or something else?"
  • Ask every person in the group individually. Don't assume based on one person's answer.
  • Bringing more than two drinks? Use a tray.
  • Make sure tables and courts have napkins and utensils.
  • On courts, wait for players to be between games or points before you approach. Check the RallyTrack TV screens for the score.
  • Clear empty cups and plates as you go.

    Ongoing check-ins

    • Check-ins are constant, not on a timer. Refresh waters, tidy the lounge, show your face.
    • If they say "not at the moment," check back in a few minutes rather than pushing.
    • If the group clearly isn't ordering, let them know about the call button.

    End of reservation

    • Every court tab needs a card on file, and is closed out 5 minutes before the reservation ends.
    • If they'd like to stay, offer to move them to a table or Garden Pod, and transfer the tab instead of closing it.
    • Tell them about the Courts & Coffee and Rally Social promo.
    • During peak time, if a court has no one booked right after, let the Captain or a manager know so they can offer the group an extension. Off-peak, just let them keep playing — no need to formally extend.

    "Wanted to let you know we're running a promotion on our Courts & Coffee and Rally Social program — 50% off for all court reservations. Courts & Coffee is our most popular morning social, rotational play with a coffee included. Social is the evening equivalent, also with a beverage included in the ticket. If you're interested, I can get you signed up at the Host Desk."
