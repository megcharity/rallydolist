Hand-Holding Hospitality is how we make every guest feel welcomed, guided, and valued, every single shift. It's the reason guests choose Rally and keep coming back.

Every guest, every time

Service is what you do. Hospitality is how you make people feel.

Hand-Holding Hospitality is service and genuine warmth working together, so every guest feels guided, cared for, and glad they chose to be here.

Magic Moments

Notice, act, follow up

Three steps

  1. Spot the opportunityWatch for milestones, needs, or issues: a birthday, a first-time guest, a problem with an order.
  2. Take actionAdd a personal touch, fix the issue quickly, or go above and beyond.
  3. Follow upCheck back in to make sure the guest is happy and feels cared for.

Offense and defense

OffenseCreating delight before anyone asks

  • A regular sits down and their usual is already on its way.
  • You introduce a new guest to a regular.
  • You notice a group celebrating and bring something unexpected.

DefenseRecovering when something goes wrong

  • A guest's food comes out wrong. You fix it fast and follow up.
  • A guest looks unhappy. You acknowledge it right away and take ownership.

When It Goes Wrong

Mistakes happen. How we recover is what guests remember. We use the four A's, in this order.

  1. ApologizeSay sorry for how it affected them, even if it wasn't our fault. No excuses.
  2. AcknowledgeShow you understand. If the guest feels there's a problem, there is one.
  3. ActTell a manager right away, fix it fast, and loop in anyone who needs to know.
  4. AssessGo back to the guest. Do they feel taken care of now?
Always involve a manager, immediately

Don't try to handle it quietly on your own, and don't wait to see if it blows over. Bring in a manager right away, then stay involved. The guest should see the whole team taking care of them.

Find a Way to Say Yes

Look for the solution first. "No" is the last resort, not the default.

This is saying yes

  • Extending a reservation for free when no other guests are waiting.
  • Going off-menu for a guest after checking with the kitchen.
  • Walking a guest to the restroom instead of pointing.

This isn't

  • Turning down a reservation extension because "the system doesn't allow it."

We don't wait to be asked. We anticipate needs and step in before guests have to ask.

The Details

A guest-ready space tells guests we care before we say a word. Keep it up all shift, not just at close.

  • Wipe down surfaces during a reservation, not just after.
  • Bus empty dishes immediately. Don’t let used plates, glasses, or baskets sit.
  • Straighten barstools and chairs as you pass.
  • Make sure every table is clean and properly set with cutlery.
  • Keep water filled on every court. Guests should not have to ask.
  • Straighten pillows and furniture so your section stays guest-ready.
  • Sweep your section and stay ahead of visible debris.

Bring the Energy

You're the Cruise Director. Guests take their cue from you.

Positivity is a professional standard, not a personality trait.

Everyone has hard days. When you're on the floor, you hold the same standard anyway. Your energy sets the tone for the whole room and for your teammates too.
