Part 1

Court & Lounge Care

These apply at all times, in every type of service. Already read the Non-Negotiables? Good — here's the rest of the foundations.

Court etiquette

Wait for players to be between games or points before you approach their court. Don't interrupt them mid-play.

Check the RallyTrack TV screens to see the score and time your visit.

Lounge care

Lounges are your responsibility for the entire reservation. This isn't a one-time step; it happens continuously.

No empty drinks or plates are left in the lounges. Clear them as you go.

Court Reservations

The flow of a standard court reservation, start to finish. Make great experiences on and off the court.

  1. 1

    Arrive & welcome

    Tap to see the steps

    Arrive & welcome

    Set the tone and get them started on the menu and the tech.

    1. Confirm via radio that you heard the Host's call that your court is being walked over.
    2. Meet the reservation at the court. If you're running food or drinks to another court and can't be there at the handoff, get there within 2 minutes of drop-off.
    3. Welcome the players and introduce yourself.

    "Hey everyone, welcome to your court! I'm [name], I'll be taking care of you today."

    Then ask yourself: are the guests new to Rally?

    Yes, new guests
    • Give a quick orientation: a rundown on things to know, show them the restrooms, and let them know they can put personal items in the cubbies.
    • Walk through the menu. Explain the sections, call out good court snacks and crushable drinks, and mention the food is really good and made with fresh ingredients.
    • Show them RallyTrack. Sign them up and guide them through the full experience: choose avatars, and if it's a large group, only add four players for the demo. Take it back to the start so they can warm up.
    No, returning guests
    • Welcome them back.
    • Mention something you're loving on the menu right now.
    • Point out a few great court snacks and crushable drinks.
    • Make sure they know how to play with RallyTrack. Answer questions and share what's new.

    Either way, try to get a drink order right away.

    Ongoing check-ins

    Ongoing check-ins

    Check-ins are constant, not on a fixed timer.

    • You don't always need to ask for an order. Straighten pillows, refresh drinks, tidy the lounge. Just show your face.
    • If you didn't get a drink order at the welcome, ask again. Read the room: if they say "not at the moment," check back in a few minutes rather than pushing.
    • If the group clearly isn't ordering and has said so, tell them about the call button. They can press it any time to order food or ask a question.
    Optional: complimentary chips (ask your MOD first)If a court looks like they aren't going to order anything, you can run complimentary chips to the court. Let them know they're on the house. It's a little nudge that often gets the group ordering.

    Find something to talk about, then ask: "How are you liking the technology?"

    "Can I get anyone a drink?"

    10 minutes before the reservation ends

    Return to check on the group and let them know you can reserve a table for them afterward.

    Check with your MODOur retention special/perk changes from time to time. Check with your MOD on what's currently running before you offer it.

    "Hi everyone, just checking on you to make sure you have everything, and to let you know we're running a promotion on [current retention perk — ask your MOD]. I can also answer any questions on our leagues if you're interested."

    If they sign up day of: "You can meet me at the host desk to learn more after you play."

  3. 3

    End of reservation

    Tap to see the steps

    End of reservation

    Peak time: if the Host radios that no one's booked on your court right after this reservation, find out and offer the group an extension before they wrap up. Off-peak: you don't need to track or offer a formal extension — just let them keep playing and let them know it's on the house.

    Five minutes before the end, check on the group and offer to transfer their tab to a table or Garden Pod if they'd like to hang out after court time.

    Yes, they're staying
    • Transfer the tab to the new table immediately. Don't close it out.
    • If they have food, move it to their new spot.
    • If they don't have food, still offer to walk them over to the table.
    No, they're leaving
    • Close out their tab 5 minutes before the reservation ends.
    • Thank them for playing and invite them back.
    • Tell them about our current retention special/perk (check with your MOD).

    As they wrap up and close out, share our current retention special/perk — check with your MOD on what's running:

    "Wanted to let you know we're running a promotion on [current retention perk — ask your MOD]. If you're interested, I can get you signed up at the Host Desk."

Leagues & Programming

League players are here to play and have a different priority. It's still your job to take care of them and give them the best service.

MandatoryLeague players get the same level of care as any other guest. We do not neglect them.
  1. 1

    Read the room & get ready

    Tap to see the steps

    Read the room & get ready

    • If players are on the court, wait for them to come off before approaching.
    • Leagues usually have players waiting in the lounges. That's your opening.
    • Make sure towels are readily available.
    • Make sure the water is filled up.
    Tap to flip back
  2. 2

    The league lap, every 7 minutes

    Tap to see the steps

    The league lap, every 7 minutes

    Instead of vaguely "checking on the league," you have a defined lap.

    A lap means: walk all four courts and lounges, pick up empties, clear trash, reset furniture, collect abandoned glassware, and look for obvious service opportunities.

    The selling happens within the lap. Because you're visibly clearing and resetting, your presence has a purpose. That reads very differently than walking up every few minutes just to ask about drinks.

    "Hey guys, can I get anyone a beer or a smoothie?"

    Read signals

    People bounce between courts and lounges, so don't try to track individual guests and orders. Watch for visual triggers instead.

    When you see…Do this
    An empty can or glassOffer another.
    Someone sitting down after a gameCheck in.
    A group gathering in a loungeOffer to grab drinks.
    Someone looking at the menuEngage.
    Food arrivingAsk the group if anyone else wants food.

  4. 4

    Swarm moments

    Tap to see the steps

    Swarm moments

    Rather than selling constantly all league long, lean hard into 2–3 key windows.

    Arrival window

    Be highly visible around the lounges as players arrive.

    "Hey guys, I'm taking care of league tonight. Let me grab you a drink before you start — what can I get you?"

    Mid-league

    Do a full sweep of all four courts and lounges. This is your strongest second-drink opportunity. Keep the offer simple and specific to what league players actually order, built around a short go-to list:

    • Light beer
    • Canned cocktails
    • Spritzes
    • NA beer

    "Want a High Noon, a Mich Ultra, or a Rally Marg?"

The manager walks the POC (point of contact) through the event, but it's still your job to know the BEO and the offering. Tick items off as you go.
